---
A recorded talk is a ready-made blog post waiting to be written.

**What to do**

1. Attach (or link) the talk recording or transcript to this issue.
2. Apply the `recycle:blog` label to kick off the recycler.
3. The recycler drafts a blog post in the repo's configured voice and opens a PR for review.

**Why this template exists**

Talks reach the people in the room; the blog post reaches everyone who searches later. One source, two audiences.
