Your best blog post deserves a second life in the inbox.

**What to do**

1. Link the published blog post (or paste its URL) in this issue.
2. Apply the `recycle:newsletter` label.
3. The recycler condenses it into a newsletter-ready issue — subject line, intro hook, and a clear call to action — and opens a PR.

**Why this template exists**

Search traffic and subscriber attention are different channels. Re-cut the same idea for the reader who already opted in.
