---
The sharpest 45 seconds of a 30-minute video is the part most people will ever see.

**What to do**

1. Link the long-form video (or attach the file) in this issue.
2. Apply the `recycle:short` label.
3. The recycler proposes the strongest clip with a caption and hook, then opens a PR with the short.

**Why this template exists**

Shorts are the trailer for the full video. One upload feeds every vertical-video surface.
