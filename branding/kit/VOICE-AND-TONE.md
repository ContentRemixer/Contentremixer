# ContentRemixer — Voice & Tone (one-pager)

> **Create once. Publish everywhere.**
> We talk like a DevRel who ships: clear, a little playful, never hype.

## Who we are
ContentRemixer is the booth where one piece of reference content becomes many. The voice
borrows the **music-production metaphor** that runs through the whole product — crate, sample,
mix, mastering, the drop — but it never lets the metaphor get in the way of a reader who just
wants to get something done.

## The three dials

| Dial | Where we sit | Sounds like |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Serious ⟷ Playful** | 65% playful | "Crate-dig your best talk, remix it into a week of posts." |
| **Formal ⟷ Casual** | 70% casual | Contractions yes. Slang sparingly. Second person ("you"). |
| **Expert ⟷ Approachable** | Expert, worn lightly | We know the pipeline cold; we explain it like a teammate, not a spec. |

## Do
- **Lead with the outcome.** Say what the reader gets before how it works.
- **Use the metaphor as flavor, not scaffolding.** One music term per paragraph, max.
- **Short sentences carry the load.** Vary rhythm; let one long sentence breathe between short ones.
- **Name the steps in product language** — *Sample → Mix → Mastering → The Drop* — then gloss them once.
- **Respect the reader's time.** Answer-first. TL;DR at the top of anything over ~400 words.
- **Be specific.** "Opens a PR with the remixed content" beats "streamlines your workflow."

## Don't
- **No hype words:** _revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, unlock, supercharge, effortless, 10x._
- **Don't over-DJ it.** If a sentence needs a decoder ring for the metaphor, cut the metaphor.
- **No em-dash pile-ups or AI throat-clearing** ("In today's fast-paced world…", "It's worth noting…").
- **Don't promise autonomy we don't ship.** The human reviews and merges every Mix.
- **Never leak internals in published copy:** no tenant IDs, tokens, IPs, or internal hostnames.

## The lexicon (say / don't say)
| Say | Instead of |
|-----|-----------|
| The Crate | the content repo / knowledge base |
| Sample | source snippet / excerpt |
| Mix · Remix | generated output / the AI result |
| Mastering | the quality/brand-voice check |
| Sample clearance | source attribution |
| The Booth | the admin web UI |
| The Drop | publishing / go-live |
| The Weekly Remix | the newsletter |

## Micro-copy patterns
- **Button:** verbs in product voice — *Spin a Mix*, *Open the Booth*, *Clear the sample*.
- **Empty state:** warm + directive — *"Crate's empty. Point the scraper at a talk to start digging."*
- **Error:** plain + recoverable — *"That source didn't clear. Check the link and try again."*
- **Success:** quiet confidence — *"Mix opened as PR #123. Give it a listen before you merge."*

## One-line test
Before you ship a sentence, ask: *would a DevRel engineer say this to a peer over coffee?*
If it sounds like a press release, remix it.
