# Human voice review skill

## Vocabulary red flags (AI tells)
delve | moreover | furthermore | in conclusion | it is important to note | leverage (as verb) | seamless | robust | game-changer | unlock | elevate | embark | navigate (metaphorical) | tapestry | landscape (metaphorical) | realm | "In today's fast-paced world" | "Look no further" | "Whether you're X or Y"

## Structural tells
- Uniform paragraph lengths; perfectly parallel triads everywhere ("X, Y, and Z" x5)
- Zero first person, zero opinions, zero specific numbers
- Hollow intensifiers: "incredibly powerful", "highly efficient"
- Em-dash overuse; semicolons in casual content; title-case subheads in a casual piece
- Scaffolding leaked into prose: visible bold labels like `**Conclusion:**`, `**Setup**`, `**Action**`, `**Result**`, `**So what:**` inside paragraphs — these are writer's internal frame, never reader-visible in a human-authored post
- Identical sub-structure repeated in every section (e.g. every H2 body has the same 4 bold sub-labels in the same order)

## The pulse test (read aloud)
- Sentence length should VARY (4-25 words). If rhythm is flat, rewrite
- Would you say this sentence to a colleague? If no, rewrite it

## Rewrite protocol
1. Cut 20% (there is always 20% of filler)
2. Replace generic claims with specifics: numbers, names, mistakes, costs
3. Add first-person experience where true ("we lost an afternoon to this")
4. Vary rhythm: split long sentences, merge fragments
5. Re-run flag scan: target < 2 flags per 1000 words

## Scoring
- Count flags per 1000 words: 0-2 pass | 3-5 rewrite pass | 5+ full rewrite
- Attach the score + flag list to the review comment
